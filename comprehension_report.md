## What this proposes

This design proposes `StandaloneQuantumBornGPT`, a 12-layer standalone Complex Quantum Born GPT trained end-to-end from random initialization on FineWeb shards without classical teacher distillation or periodic ridge-regression refits. To prevent quadratic density-matrix attention from erasing token sign and complex phase, each layer fuses an isometric $\sqrt{2}$-packed Rank-4 Hermitian density matrix (`256` dims) with a first-moment coherent value ket (`32` dims) into a single `288`-width causal attention pass, purifies the mixed state via 1-step McWeeny squaring, and extracts an Uhlmann phase-locked Rank-2 Lanczos-Krylov eigenpair inside a `@torch.compile` helper to form a `320`-dim/head observable vector. All intermediate Stiefel projections and complex head unitaries are updated directly via Tikhonov-damped `RiemannianStiefelMuon`, bypassing 3-factor SVD readouts. Finally, the design introduces two active-token vocabulary manifold optimizers (`BatchSubspaceTikhonovGramMuon` and `RiemannianSphereTangentSNROptimizer`) restricted strictly to tokens present in the current batch, and benchmarks them alongside `AdamW` across a Rank-2 Krylov Born vocabulary readout and a softcap linear Cross-Entropy baseline on `10,485,760` FineWeb validation tokens.

## How the existing system works, per this document

The existing Wave 47 model ([`RiemannianImpureDensityGPT`](file:///home/nixos/QuantumDLBlocks/src/qdlblocks/models/riemannian_impure_density_gpt.py)) maps token values exclusively into `256`-dimensional quadratic Hermitian outer products $\rho = \sum_{r=1}^R p_r |\hat{v}^{(r)}\rangle\langle\hat{v}^{(r)}|$ before causal attention and seeds its Rank-2 Lanczos-Krylov extraction from a real diagonal vector, which destroys linear sign and $U(1)$ complex phase information. Rather than training end-to-end from scratch, the Wave 47 verification script initializes token embeddings (`wte`) and the language model head (`lm_head`) from a `400M`-token pre-trained [`CompiledLeaderboardClassicalGPT`](file:///home/nixos/QuantumDLBlocks/src/qdlblocks/models/compiled_leaderboard_classical_gpt.py) teacher checkpoint. Furthermore, its internal readout layers are parameterized as 3-factor SVD matrices ($U \operatorname{diag}(\sigma) V^\top$) that are excluded from `AllLayerRiemannianStiefelMuon` because unweighted Newton-Schulz orthogonalization amplifies small tail singular values ($\sigma_{768} \approx 10^{-4}$) by $10^4\times$; instead, those readouts are frozen during gradient steps and periodically overwritten every 20 steps via Golub-Pereyra polar ridge regression against cached teacher activations. At the vocabulary boundary, existing options either freeze random Step-0 embeddings on the sphere (preventing token geometry learning) or apply dense $50,304 \times 768$ Newton-Schulz orthogonalization across all tokens, contaminating roughly `45,000` inactive tokens per batch and amplifying rare-token gradient noise.
