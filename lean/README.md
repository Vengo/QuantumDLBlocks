## Detailed Mathematical Summary of Each Module

### 1. Theorem 1 — Completeness of the Quotient Horizontal Projection & False-Symmetry Detection (`QuotientHorizontalCompleteness.lean`)
- **Theorem 1(a) (`theorem1a_chain_rule_horizontal_completeness`)**: Given an inner-product tangent space $T$ decomposed orthogonally into a vertical gauge subspace $V = \operatorname{Im}(\Pi_{\mathcal{V}})$ and horizontal complement $H = \operatorname{Im}(\Pi_{\mathcal{H}})$ ($\Pi_{\mathcal{H}}(v) = v - \Pi_{\mathcal{V}}(v)$), if the forward differential $df : T \to W$ is gauge-invariant along $V$ ($\forall v,\; df(\Pi_{\mathcal{V}} v) = 0$), then:
  1. $df(\Pi_{\mathcal{H}} v) = df(v)$ for all $v \in T$, and
  2. Every loss pullback gradient $g \in T$ satisfies $\Pi_{\mathcal{V}} g = 0$, $\Pi_{\mathcal{H}} g = g$, and $\|\Pi_{\mathcal{H}} g\|^2 = \|g\|^2$ (`100%` horizontal gradient retention).
- **Theorem 1(b) (`theorem1b_false_symmetry_vertical_leak_and_norm_loss`)**: Conversely, if a candidate projection $\Pi_{\mathcal{V}}'$ is a *false symmetry* ($\exists v_0 \in T,\; df(\Pi_{\mathcal{V}}' v_0) \neq 0$), then there exists a linear loss whose pullback gradient $g$ suffers strictly non-zero vertical leakage $\Pi_{\mathcal{V}}' g \neq 0$ and strict norm loss $\|\Pi_{\mathcal{H}}' g\|^2 < \|g\|^2$ (explaining the `69.8%` vertical gradient leak diagnosed in Wave 66 `W66_G3`).

### 2. Theorem 2 — Split-Spectrum Biorthogonal SVD Normal-Space Decomposition (`SplitSpectrumBiorthogonalSVD.lean`)
- Formalizes the local $(i, j)$ coordinate differential $(U^\top dM V)_{ij} = \sigma_j (\Omega_U)_{ij} - \sigma_i (\Omega_V)_{ij}$ of $M(U, \sigma, V) = U \operatorname{diag}(\sigma) V^\top$ under vertical skew-symmetric perturbations $(\Omega_U, \Omega_V) \in \mathfrak{so}(r) \times \mathfrak{so}(r)$.
- **Theorem 2(a) (`theorem2a_degenerate_spectrum_synchronous_spin`)**: When $\sigma_i = \sigma_j = \sigma \neq 0$, the vertical kernel condition is equivalent to exact synchronous co-rotation $(\Omega_U)_{ij} = (\Omega_V)_{ij}$.
- **Theorem 2(b) & Corollary 2(c) (`theorem2b_split_spectrum_zero_vertical_spin`, `theorem2c_positive_split_spectrum_physical_spin`)**: When $\sigma_i^2 \neq \sigma_j^2$ (in particular when $0 < \sigma_i \neq \sigma_j > 0$), the coupled skew-symmetric system forces $(\Omega_U)_{ij} = 0 \wedge (\Omega_V)_{ij} = 0$. Any non-zero spin on split singular values alters the physical operator $M$, proving why `project_coupled_svd_raw_exact_degenerate` must mask by $|\sigma_i - \sigma_j| = 0$.

### 3. Theorem 3 — CPTP Kraus $\mathfrak{u}(R)$ Gauge Invariance & Sylvester Horizontal Condition (`KrausUnitaryGaugeSylvester.lean`)
- **Theorem 3(a) (`theorem3a_kraus_unitary_channel_and_tp_invariance`, `theorem3a_two_branch_direct_expansion`)**: Mixing Kraus operators $K'_r = \sum_{s=1}^R W_{rs} K_s$ by a unitary $R \times R$ matrix $W$ ($\sum_r \overline{W_{ra}} W_{rb} = \delta_{ab}$) leaves both the CPTP quantum channel $\mathcal{E}_{\mathbf{K}}(\rho) = \sum_r K_r \rho K_r^\dagger$ and the Stiefel trace-preservation sum $\sum_r K_r^\dagger K_r$ identically invariant.
- **Theorem 3(b) (`theorem3b_kraus_sylvester_horizontal_iff`)**: For skew-Hermitian $\Omega \in \mathfrak{u}(R)$, the horizontal decomposition $\Xi_{\text{horiz}, r} = \Xi_r - \sum_s \Omega_{rs} K_s$ is orthogonal to the vertical $\mathfrak{u}(R)$ gauge fiber iff $\Omega$ satisfies the Sylvester/Lyapunov equation $\sum_s (\Omega_{rs} M_{st} + M_{rs} \Omega_{st}) = \langle \Xi_r, K_t\rangle - \langle K_r, \Xi_t\rangle$ where $M_{rs} = \langle K_r, K_s\rangle$.

### 4. Theorem 4 — Non-Invariance of Horizontal Subspaces Under Odd-Degree Newton–Schulz Polynomials (`NewtonSchulzHorizontalLeakage.lean`)
- **Theorem 4(a) (`theorem4a_newton_schulz_horizontal_leakage`)**: Provides an explicit machine-checked $2 \times 2$ witness $K = \operatorname{diag}(1, 2)$, $\Xi = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $G = \Xi^\top \Xi = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 3 \\ 3 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ where $\Xi$ is strictly horizontal ($\Pi_{\mathcal{V}}(\Xi) = \Xi K^\top - K \Xi^\top = 0$), yet $[G, K^\top K] \neq 0$, $\Pi_{\mathcal{V}}(\Xi G) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -3 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \neq 0$, and $\Pi_{\mathcal{V}}(\Phi_{a,b,c}(\Xi)) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -3b - 21c \\ 3b + 21c & 0 \end{pmatrix} \neq 0$ for all odd-degree Newton–Schulz polynomials with $b + 7c \neq 0$.
- **Theorem 4(b) (`theorem4b_post_ns_horizontal_projection_restores_horizontality`)**: Proves that re-applying $\Pi_{\mathcal{H}}$ after $\Phi(\Xi)$ restores exact horizontality $\Pi_{\mathcal{V}}(\Pi_{\mathcal{H}}(\Phi(\Xi))) = 0$.

- **Theorem 5(a) & 5(b) (`theorem5a_luders_joint_nonneg`, `theorem5b_luders_exact_bipartite_normalization`)**: For any bipartite vocabulary $V = A \times B$ ($256_{\text{coarse}} \times 256_{\text{fine}} = 65,536$), coarse Born probabilities $p_a \ge 0$ ($\sum_{a=1}^A p_a = 1$) and conditional fine Born probabilities $q_{b|a} \ge 0$ ($\sum_{b=1}^B q_{b|a} = 1$) define a valid joint distribution $P(a, b) = p_a q_{b|a} \ge 0$ with exact partition-free normalization $\sum_{a=1}^A \sum_{b=1}^B p_a q_{b|a} = 1$.
- **Theorem 5(c) (`theorem5c_complementary_subspace_parseval_norm_preservation`)**: For complementary orthogonal frames $[U_{\text{coarse}}, U_{\text{fine}}] \in \operatorname{O}(2d)$, $\|U_{\text{coarse}}^\top u\|^2 + \|U_{\text{fine}}^\top u\|^2 = \|u\|^2$ (`100%` Parseval norm preservation across the two $d$-dimensional stages, vs. `50%` norm loss in a single-stage rank-$d$ projection on balanced states).

### 6. Theorem 6 — Hilbert–Schmidt Born Similarity Positivity, Isometric Outer-Product Flattening, & $U(R)$-Gauge Invariance (`HilbertSchmidtQuantumAttention.lean`)
- **Theorem 6(a) (`theorem6a_hilbert_schmidt_born_nonneg_and_vacuum_norm`)**: Proves that for Cholesky/Purification factors $Q, K$ with outer-product density matrices $\rho_Q = Q Q^\top$ and $\rho_K = K K^\top$, the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_Q \rho_K) = \|Q^\top K\|_F^2 \ge 0$ is strictly non-negative without `exp`/`softmax`, and any sequence of non-negative similarities $S_1,\dots,S_n \ge 0$ with positive vacuum floor $\varepsilon > 0$ has a strictly positive denominator $D = \sum_j S_j + \varepsilon > 0$ and exact partition-free normalization.
- **Theorem 6(b) (`theorem6b_isometric_outer_product_flattening`)**: Proves that the packed symmetric inner product $(\rho_Q)_{00}(\rho_K)_{00} + (\rho_Q)_{11}(\rho_K)_{11} + 2(\rho_Q)_{01}(\rho_K)_{01}$ equals $\langle \rho_Q, \rho_K \rangle_F = \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_Q \rho_K) = \|Q^\top K\|_F^2$.

### 7. Theorem 7 — Quantum "OR + AND" Density-Matrix Closure via Convex Superposition and the Schur Product Theorem (`QuantumOrAndSchurLogic.lean`)
- **Theorem 7(a) (`theorem7a_quantum_or_convex_superposition_closure`)**: Proves that for factored PSD Hermitian density matrices $\rho_A = L_A L_A^\top \succeq 0$ and $\rho_B = L_B L_B^\top \succeq 0$ and non-negative weights $w_A, w_B \ge 0$, the Quantum "OR" state $w_A \rho_A + w_B \rho_B$ is Hermitian, positive semidefinite ($\forall \psi,\; \langle \psi \mid (w_A \rho_A + w_B \rho_B) \mid \psi \rangle \ge 0$), and satisfies $\operatorname{Tr}(w_A \rho_A + w_B \rho_B) = w_A \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_A) + w_B \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_B) \ge 0$.
- **Theorem 7(b) (`schur_khatri_rao_entry_factorization`, `theorem7b_quantum_and_schur_product_theorem`)**: Proves the **Schur Product Theorem**: the elementwise product $(\rho_A \circ \rho_B)_{ij} = (\rho_A)_{ij}(\rho_B)_{ij}$ factors identically as the Gram outer product $L_{A \circ B} L_{A \circ B}^\top$ of the row-wise Kronecker (Khatri–Rao) factor $(L_{A \circ B})_{i,(r,s)} = (L_A)_{i,r}(L_B)_{i,s}$, so $\langle \psi \mid (\rho_A \circ \rho_B) \mid \psi \rangle = \sum_{r,s} ((L_A)_{0,r}(L_B)_{0,s}\psi_0 + (L_A)_{1,r}(L_B)_{1,s}\psi_1)^2 \ge 0$ is strictly PSD and Hermitian with non-negative coincidence trace $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_A \circ \rho_B) = \sum_i (\rho_A)_{ii}(\rho_B)_{ii} \ge 0$.
