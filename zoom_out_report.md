### Finding 1: Undecided Readout Architecture & Sparse-vs-Dense Vocabulary Gradient Contradiction
* **Severity**: **Blocker**
* **Affected Scope**: `## The Problem` (lines 31–33), `### 2.4 Active-Token Vocabulary Manifold Optimizers` (lines 184–197), and `### 2.5 Rule-9 Single-Pass Chunked Vocabulary Readouts` (lines 198–204).
* **Simplicity Rationale**:
  1. **Sparse vs. Dense Vocabulary Gradient Mismatch**: Section 2.4 restricts the vocabulary manifold optimizers (`BatchSubspaceTikhonovGramMuon` and `RiemannianSphereTangentSNROptimizer`) to active batch tokens $\mathcal{I}_{\text{act}} = \operatorname{unique}(\text{idx} \cup \text{targets})$. However, the design never specifies whether the input token embedding `wte` and the output vocabulary readout `lm_head` (or $E \in \mathbb{R}^{V \times 768}$) are weight-tied or untied, or which matrix the active-token manifold optimizers step:
     * In both `RANK2_KRYLOV_BORN` and `SOFTCAP_LINEAR_CE`, the loss partition function sums over all $V = 50,304$ vocabulary rows, producing **dense** non-zero gradients across all $50,304$ rows of the readout matrix at every step.
     * Conversely, an untied input `wte` receives non-zero gradients **only** on $\operatorname{unique}(\text{idx})$ (never on $\text{targets} \setminus \text{idx}$).
     * If `wte` and `lm_head` are weight-tied and updated only on $\mathcal{I}_{\text{act}}$, the optimizer silently discards the repulsive partition-function gradient on the `~45,000` non-target vocabulary rows. If `wte` and `lm_head` are untied (as in [`RiemannianImpureDensityGPT`](file:///home/nixos/QuantumDLBlocks/src/qdlblocks/models/riemannian_impure_density_gpt.py#L706-L732)), `wte.grad` is zero on $\text{targets} \setminus \text{idx}$ (so $\mathcal{I}_{\text{act}}$ should be $\operatorname{unique}(\text{idx})$) and the optimizer governing `lm_head.weight` is left unspecified.
  2. **Unresolved Architectural Branching in `RANK2_KRYLOV_BORN`**: Section 2.5 leaves the core readout representation undecided—stating that `Rank2KrylovBornChunkedCEFn` *"Maps the final normalized hidden state $h_t \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$ into a Rank-2 complex measurement state pair $(|\Psi_{1,t}\rangle, |\Psi_{2,t}\rangle) \in \mathbb{C}^{384} \times \mathbb{C}^{384}$ (or two real measurement branches in $\mathbb{R}^{768}$) weighted by normalized Krylov eigenvalues $(\hat{\lambda}_{1,t}, \hat{\lambda}_{2,t})$"*—without specifying how $h_t \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$ yields two branches and $(\hat{\lambda}_{1,t}, \hat{\lambda}_{2,t})$ or what the exact calibrated log-Born logit formula is. Lock a single concrete readout representation and explicitly specify the `wte` vs. `lm_head` parameter/optimizer assignment.

### Finding 4: Confounded SNR Gating inside `BatchSubspaceTikhonovGramMuon` & Contradictory Attention Weight Formula
* **Severity**: **Major**
* **Affected Scope**: `### 1. System Data Flow & Block Architecture` (line 101) and `### 2.4 Active-Token Vocabulary Manifold Optimizers` (lines 186–194).
* **Simplicity Rationale**:
  * **Confounded Optimizer Ablation (YAGNI)**: In Section 2.4, `BatchSubspaceTikhonovGramMuon` applies both $768 \times 768$ Tikhonov-damped feature Gram Newton-Schulz preconditioning ($A^{-1/2}$) **and** an *"adaptive median SNR trust-region gate per active token row"* (line 192), while `RiemannianSphereTangentSNROptimizer` tests per-row SNR trust-region gating $\text{SNR}_v / (\text{SNR}_v + \tau_{\text{snr}})$ on $\sqrt{d}\,\mathbb{S}^{d-1}$. Bolting a second, different SNR gate onto `BatchSubspaceTikhonovGramMuon` over-engineers the optimizer and confounds Hypothesis H3's comparison between feature-Gram preconditioning and per-row SNR gating. Keep `BatchSubspaceTikhonovGramMuon` cleanly focused on active-subspace Tikhonov Gram orthogonalization + sphere retraction.
  * **Attention Score Contradiction**: Line 101 of the architecture diagram writes `Causal Born / Softmax-Aligned Weights W_ts,h = Softmax_s(Scale * Re⟨q_t | k_s⟩)`, which drops the imaginary part of $\langle q_t \mid k_s \rangle_{\mathbb{C}}$ and contradicts the quartic `exp`-free causal Born attention $w_{ts,h} \propto |\langle q_t \mid k_s \rangle_{\mathbb{C}}|^4$ used in [`RiemannianImpureDensityBlock.compute_packed_density_features`](file:///home/nixos/QuantumDLBlocks/src/qdlblocks/models/riemannian_impure_density_gpt.py#L429-L436) and [`fused_causal_pboson_born_triton`](file:///home/nixos/QuantumDLBlocks/src/qdlblocks/kernels/flash_complex_krylov_triton.py), while Section 2 omits the causal attention score equation altogether. Specify the exact causal attention weight equation in Section 2 and align the diagram in Section 1.
