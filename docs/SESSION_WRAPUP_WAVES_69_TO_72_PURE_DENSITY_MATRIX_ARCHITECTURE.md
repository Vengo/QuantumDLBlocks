### 1.1 What `QuantumDLBlocks` Is Trying to Accomplish
`QuantumDLBlocks` is a research codebase developing **quantum-mechanical deep learning architectures** for autoregressive language modeling. Instead of using standard classical Transformer blocks (real-valued residual vectors $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^d$, `exp`/`softmax` dot-product attention, unconstrained linear layers, and Euclidean AdamW optimization), we formulate sequence modeling in the mathematical language of **open quantum systems** and **Riemannian manifold optimization**:

1. **Quantum State Representation**: Tokens and contextual histories are represented as **complex quantum states**—specifically **density matrices** $\rho \in \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^{d_h})$ (Hermitian, positive semi-definite matrices $\rho = \rho^\dagger \succeq 0$ with unit trace $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho) = 1$) that generalize pure wavefunctions $|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ to statistical mixtures capable of representing polysemy, contextual uncertainty, and multi-qubit entanglement.
2. **Physical Evolution Channels**: Layers evolve states through **Completely Positive Trace-Preserving (CPTP) quantum channels** $\mathcal{E}(\rho) = \sum_{r=1}^R K_r \rho K_r^\dagger$ (where the Kraus operators $K_r \in \mathbb{C}^{d_h \times d_h}$ satisfy the trace-preservation identity $\sum_{r=1}^R K_r^\dagger K_r = I_{d_h}$), unitary evolutions $U \rho U^\dagger$ ($U \in SU(d_h)$), and **Hilbert–Schmidt quantum similarity** $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^Q \rho^K) \ge 0$ without `exp` or `softmax` in the attention kernel.
3. **Quantum Measurement Readout**: Next-token probabilities $P(x_{t+1} = v \mid x_{\le t})$ over the $V = 50,257$ vocabulary are extracted from the final-layer density states $\rho_{t,h}^{(L)}$ via **Born's rule** (where a positive operator-valued measure / POVM effect $E_v \succeq 0$ yields probability $P(v) \propto \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_t^{(L)} E_v)$) and a **Two-Stage Sequential Lüders Quantum Instrument** (a conditional two-step quantum measurement over coarse and fine token sub-indices defined mathematically in Section 2.2).
4. **Riemannian Quotient Optimization**: All operator weights live on exact compact manifolds—the **real Stiefel manifold** $\operatorname{St}(n, p) = \{W \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times p} : W^\top W = I_p\}$, the **special unitary group** $SU(16) = \{U \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 16} : U^\dagger U = I_{16},\, \det(U) = 1\}$, and the **CPTP Kraus Stiefel manifold** $\operatorname{St}_{\mathbb{C}}(R d_h, d_h)$—and are updated along **horizontal quotient geodesics** (removing unphysical vertical gauge degrees of freedom that alter weight entries without changing the forward density matrix).

### 2.1 Dual Representation & Lockstep Synchronization of Density States $(L_{t,h}^{(\ell)}, \tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell)})$
At every token position $t \in \{1 \dots T\}$ and layer $\ell \in \{0 \dots L\}$, the network state consists of $H = 16$ heads, each holding a $16 \times 16$ complex density matrix $\rho_{t,h}^{(\ell)} \in \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^{16})$ ($d_h = 16$). Rather than storing a raw $16 \times 16$ complex matrix and running expensive $O(d_h^3)$ eigendecompositions to enforce $\rho \succeq 0$, each layer maintains a **coupled two-rail quantum representation**:

1. **Coherent Low-Rank Cholesky / Purification Carrier $L_{t,h}^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times R}$ ($R = 4$)**:
   - Represents a Rank-$4$ density state $\rho_{\text{chol},t,h}^{(\ell)} = L_{t,h}^{(\ell)} (L_{t,h}^{(\ell)})^\dagger$. Normalizing its Frobenius norm $\|L_{t,h}^{(\ell)}\|_F = \sqrt{\operatorname{Tr}(L L^\dagger)} = 1$ via `normalize_cholesky_factor_frobenius` guarantees $\rho_{\text{chol},t,h}^{(\ell)} \succeq 0$, $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_{\text{chol},t,h}^{(\ell)}) = 1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\rho_{\text{chol},t,h}^{(\ell)}) \le 4$ by construction.
2. **Full-Rank (up to Rank 16) Isometric Packed Hermitian Vector $\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell)} = \tilde{P}(\rho_{t,h}^{(\ell)}) \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$**:
   - Any Hermitian matrix $\rho \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 16}$ has $16$ real diagonal entries $\rho_{ii} \in \mathbb{R}$ and $120$ strictly upper-triangular complex entries $\rho_{ij}$ ($1 \le i < j \le 16$). The isometric packing map $\tilde{P} : \operatorname{Herm}(\mathbb{C}^{16}) \to \mathbb{R}^{256}$ (`fast_pack_hermitian_to_isometric_real`, with exact inverse $\tilde{P}^{-1}$ `fast_unpack_isometric_real_to_hermitian`) packs the $16$ diagonals and $\sqrt{2}\,\operatorname{Re}(\rho_{ij}), \sqrt{2}\,\operatorname{Im}(\rho_{ij})$ into $\mathbb{R}^{256}$ ($16 + 2 \times 120 = 256$).
   - Because off-diagonal pairs appear twice in $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^{(A)} \rho^{(B)}) = \sum_i \rho^{(A)}_{ii} \rho^{(B)}_{ii} + 2 \sum_{i < j} (\operatorname{Re}\rho^{(A)}_{ij} \operatorname{Re}\rho^{(B)}_{ij} + \operatorname{Im}\rho^{(A)}_{ij} \operatorname{Im}\rho^{(B)}_{ij})$, the $\sqrt{2}$ scaling makes $\tilde{P}$ an **exact Frobenius / Hilbert–Schmidt isometry**:
     $$\big\langle \tilde{P}(\rho^{(A)}),\; \tilde{P}(\rho^{(B)}) \big\rangle_{\mathbb{R}^{256}} \equiv \operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\rho^{(A)} \rho^{(B)}\right).$$
3. **How $L_{t,h}^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 4}$ and $\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$ Stay Synchronized Without Eigendecomposition**:
   - Sub-blocks that increase the rank of the density matrix beyond $R = 4$ (up to full Rank $16$)—such as causal Hilbert–Schmidt attention over $t$ positions, Multi-Branch Kraus superoperators ($M=4$ branches $\times$ Rank $4 = \text{Rank } 16$), and Schur `OR+AND` products—update **both** rails simultaneously without ever calling an eigensolver:
     - **On the full-rank $\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$ rail**: The full-rank density outputs ($\tilde{\rho}_{\text{attn}}$, $\tilde{\rho}_{\text{kraus}}$, $\tilde{\rho}_{\text{logic}}$) are combined by convex combination $\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell)} = (1 - \alpha)\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell-1)} + \alpha \tilde{\rho}_{\text{sub}}$, preserving $\rho \succeq 0$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho) = 1$ even at full Rank 16.
     - **On the Rank-4 $L_{t,h}^{(\ell)} \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 4}$ rail**: In parallel, causal attention computes the attention-weighted Value Cholesky average $\bar{V}_{t,h} = \operatorname{Norm}_F(\sum_{s \le t} A_{t,s}^{(h)} V_{s,h}) \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 4}$, and Multi-Branch / Schur stages form the Frobenius-normalized coherent superposition of their branch Cholesky factors $L_{t,h}^{(\ell)} = \operatorname{Norm}_F(\sqrt{1-\alpha}\,L_{t,h}^{(\ell-1)} + \sqrt{\alpha}\,L_{\text{sub}}) \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 4}$.
     - **Coupling Between Rails**: Inside each layer's Value preparation, the Rank-4 outer product $\tilde{P}(V_{t,h} V_{t,h}^\dagger)$ is convexly blended with the full-rank incoming state $\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell-1)}$ ($\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^V = (1 - g_v)\tilde{P}(V_{t,h} V_{t,h}^\dagger) + g_v \tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(\ell-1)}$), and at final Layer-$L$ readout, the unpacked full-rank matrix $\rho_{t,h}^{(L)} = \tilde{P}^{-1}(\tilde{\rho}_{t,h}^{(L)}) \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 16}$ acts directly on the dominant ket of $L_{t,h}^{(L)}$ via matrix-vector multiplication $\rho_{t,h}^{(L)} |\psi_{\text{dom},t,h}^{(L)}\rangle \in \mathbb{C}^{16}$.

   - At Layer $L$, the evolved density matrices $\rho_{t,h}^{(L)} \in \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^{16})$ and Cholesky factors $L_{t,h}^{(L)} \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 4}$ are measured across 3 complementary quantum channels per head:
     1. **Coherent Ket Channel**: $\rho_{t,h}^{(L)} |\psi_{\text{dom},t,h}^{(L)}\rangle \in \mathbb{C}^{16}$ (`32` real values/head $\times 16 = 512$),
     2. **MUB Born Diagonal Channel**: Born probabilities in two Mutually Unbiased Bases $U_{1,h}, U_{2,h} \in U(16)$ (`32` real values/head $\times 16 = 512$, defined in Section 2.4), and

     - *Stage 2 (Conditional Fine Measurement)*: Evaluates fine outcome probabilities $q(b \mid a, \mathbf{h}_2) = \frac{\exp(\beta_L \langle \tilde{\mathbf{h}}_{2 \mid a}, e^{\text{fine}}_b \rangle)}{\sum_{b'=0}^{255} \exp(\beta_L \langle \tilde{\mathbf{h}}_{2 \mid a}, e^{\text{fine}}_{b'} \rangle)}$ (`256`-way normalization).
     - By the chain rule of conditional probability (**Lean 4 Theorem 5**), the joint instrument probability $P_{\text{Lüders}}(a_v, b_v) = p(a_v \mid \mathbf{h}_2)\,q(b_v \mid a_v, \mathbf{h}_2)$ satisfies $\sum_{a=0}^{255} \sum_{b=0}^{255} P_{\text{Lüders}}(a, b) \equiv 1$ using only two tiny $256$-way normalizations (`512` inner products instead of `50,257`), and contributes $\log p(a_v) + \log q(b_v \mid a_v)$ directly to the token log-likelihood!

### 2.3 CPTP Kraus Stiefel Manifold & Quotient Horizontal Optimization (`PureDensityQuotientMuon`)
Every learnable matrix in the density-matrix trunk lives on one of three Riemannian quotient manifolds:

1. **Real Stiefel Manifold $\operatorname{St}(n, p) = \{W \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times p} : W^\top W = I_p\}$**: Used for cross-head mixing matrices ($512 \times 512$ and $1024 \times 512$).
2. **Special Unitary Manifold $SU(16)$ & Trace-Free $\mathfrak{su}(16)$ Horizontal Projection**:
   - Used for head unitaries $U_h \in SU(16)$ ($U_h^\dagger U_h = I_{16}$). Because conjugating a density matrix $U_h \rho U_h^\dagger$ is completely invariant to multiplying $U_h \mapsto e^{i\theta_h} U_h$ by a global phase $e^{i\theta_h} \in U(1)$, the vertical gauge direction at $U_h$ is $\mathcal{V}_{U_h} = \{i \theta U_h : \theta \in \mathbb{R}\}$.
   - Given a complex Euclidean gradient $G_h \in \mathbb{C}^{16 \times 16}$, `project_su16_horizontal_tangent` first projects $G_h$ onto the unitary tangent space $A_h = \frac{1}{2}(U_h^\dagger G_h - G_h^\dagger U_h) \in \mathfrak{u}(16)$ ($A_h^\dagger = -A_h$) and then subtracts the imaginary trace component to project onto the **trace-free horizontal Lie algebra $\mathfrak{su}(16)$**:
     $$A_{h,\text{horiz}} = A_h - \frac{\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Tr}(A_h)}{16} i I_{16}, \qquad \Xi_{h,\text{horiz}} = U_h A_{h,\text{horiz}}, \qquad \operatorname{Tr}(U_h^\dagger \Xi_{h,\text{horiz}}) \equiv 0.$$
3. **CPTP Kraus Stiefel Manifold $\operatorname{St}_{\mathbb{C}}(R d_h, d_h)$ & Exact Sylvester/Lyapunov Horizontal Projection (`Lean 4 Theorem 3`)**:
   - A rank-$R$ CPTP channel $\mathcal{E}_{\mathbf{K}}(\rho) = \sum_{r=1}^R K_r \rho K_r^\dagger$ has $R$ Kraus matrices $K_1, \dots, K_R \in \mathbb{C}^{d_h \times d_h}$ satisfying $\sum_{r=1}^R K_r^\dagger K_r = I_{d_h}$. Stacking $K_1, \dots, K_R$ vertically forms a single tall matrix $\mathbf{K} = [K_1^\top, \dots, K_R^\top]^\top \in \mathbb{C}^{(R d_h) \times d_h}$ (`(H, 128, 16)` for $R=8, d_h=16$) satisfying $\mathbf{K}^\dagger \mathbf{K} = \sum_{r=1}^R K_r^\dagger K_r = I_{d_h}$—so **every CPTP Kraus channel is a point on the complex Stiefel manifold $\operatorname{St}_{\mathbb{C}}(R d_h, d_h)$**!
   - Furthermore, mixing the $R$ Kraus blocks by any unitary $W \in U(R)$ via $K'_r = \sum_{s=1}^R W_{rs} K_s$ leaves the quantum channel strictly unchanged: $\mathcal{E}_{W \cdot \mathbf{K}}(\rho) = \mathcal{E}_{\mathbf{K}}(\rho)$ (**Theorem 3a**).
   - Infinitesimally, the vertical gauge subspace at $\mathbf{K}$ consists of block-mixing perturbations $\mathcal{V}_{\mathbf{K}} = \{\delta \mathbf{K} : \delta K_r = \sum_{s=1}^R \Omega_{rs} K_s,\; \Omega^\dagger = -\Omega \in \mathfrak{u}(R)\}$.
   - **Sylvester/Lyapunov Horizontal Equation (`Theorem 3b`, `project_kraus_stiefel_horizontal_tangent`)**: Given a Stiefel tangent $\Xi = (\Xi_1, \dots, \Xi_R)$, decomposing $\Xi_r = \Xi_{\text{horiz},r} + \sum_{s=1}^R \Omega_{rs} K_s$ such that $\Xi_{\text{horiz}}$ is Frobenius-orthogonal to $\mathcal{V}_{\mathbf{K}}$ ($\sum_{r,s} \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(\Xi_{\text{horiz},r}^\dagger A_{rs} K_s) = 0\;\forall A \in \mathfrak{u}(R)$) yields the continuous Sylvester/Lyapunov equation for $\Omega \in \mathfrak{u}(R)$:
     $$M \Omega + \Omega M = G_{\text{skew}}, \qquad \text{where } M_{rs} = \operatorname{Tr}(K_r^\dagger K_s) \succeq 0, \quad (G_{\text{skew}})_{rt} = \operatorname{Tr}(\Xi_r^\dagger K_t - K_r^\dagger \Xi_t).$$
     Solving $M \Omega + \Omega M = G_{\text{skew}}$ in the eigenbasis of the small $R \times R$ Hermitian overlap matrix $M = V \operatorname{diag}(\lambda) V^\dagger$ via $(V^\dagger \Omega V)_{ij} = \frac{(V^\dagger G_{\text{skew}} V)_{ij}}{\lambda_i + \lambda_j}$ and subtracting $\sum_s \Omega_{rs} K_s$ removes 100% of the vertical $\mathfrak{u}(R)$ gauge component!
4. **Post-Newton–Schulz Horizontal Re-Projection (`Lean 4 Theorem 4`)**:
   - Odd-degree Newton–Schulz whitening polynomials $\Phi(\Xi) = a \Xi + b \Xi (\Xi^\dagger \Xi) + c \Xi (\Xi^\dagger \Xi)^2$ do not preserve $\mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{K}}$ because $\Xi^\dagger \Xi$ need not commute with the vertical $\mathfrak{u}(R)$ action (**Theorem 4**). `PureDensityQuotientMuon` therefore applies $\Pi_{\mathcal{H}}(\Phi(\Pi_{\mathcal{H}}(\cdot)))$ both *before* and *after* Newton–Schulz whitening.

### 2.4 Mathematical Definitions of Reported Telemetry Invariants (`MUBErr`, `SU16Phase`, `KrausGauge`, `Hot CE`, `EMA CE`)
Every training evaluation logs five diagnostic metrics:
- **`Hot CE` vs. `EMA CE`**: `Hot CE` is the validation cross-entropy of the live model weights; `EMA CE` is the validation cross-entropy of the shadow Exponential Moving Average (`decay = 0.995`) after retracting all shadow Stiefel/Unitary matrices onto their exact manifolds via Polar Express retraction.
- **`MUBErr` (Mutually Unbiased Bases Unbiasedness Error)**:
  - In `ComplementaryDualSubspace512DBoundary`, each head measures $\rho_{t,h}^{(L)} \in \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^{16})$ in two **Mutually Unbiased Bases (MUBs)** $U_{1,h} \in SU(16)$ and $U_{2,h} = F_{16} U_{1,h} \in U(16)$, where $(F_{16})_{jk} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{16}} e^{2\pi i j k / 16} = \frac{1}{4} e^{2\pi i j k / 16}$ is the unitary $16 \times 16$ Discrete Fourier Transform matrix.
  - Two orthonormal bases $U_{1,h}, U_{2,h}$ of $\mathbb{C}^{16}$ are *mutually unbiased* iff every basis vector of $U_{1,h}$ has uniform overlap magnitude $\frac{1}{\sqrt{16}} = 0.25$ with every basis vector of $U_{2,h}$: $|(U_{1,h}^\dagger U_{2,h})_{jk}| = 0.25\;\forall j, k \in \{0 \dots 15\}$.
  - **`MUBErr`** is the maximum departure from exact mutual unbiasedness across all 16 heads and all $16 \times 16 = 256$ basis pairs:
    $$\text{MUBErr} = \max_{h \in \{1..16\},\; j,k \in \{0..15\}} \Big| \big|(U_{1,h}^\dagger U_{2,h})_{jk}\big| - \frac{1}{4} \Big| \qquad (\text{target: } < 10^{-7};\text{ observed: } 5.0 \times 10^{-8}).$$
- **`SU16Phase` ($\mathfrak{su}(16)$ Global-Phase Vertical Leakage)**:
  - Measures the relative Frobenius norm of the unphysical $U(1)$ global-phase trace component in the horizontal update $\Xi_h \in T_{U_h} SU(16)$ across all $SU(16)$ parameters:
    $$\text{SU16Phase} = \max_h \frac{|\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Tr}(U_h^\dagger \Xi_h)|}{\sqrt{16}\,\|\Xi_h\|_F + \varepsilon} \qquad (\text{exact target & observed value: } 0.00\text{e+}00).$$
- **`KrausGauge` ($\mathfrak{u}(R)$ Kraus Block-Mixing Vertical Leakage)**:
  - Measures the fraction of the Kraus Stiefel update $\Xi_{\mathbf{K}}$ that lies in the vertical $\mathfrak{u}(R)$ block-mixing subspace $\mathcal{V}_{\mathbf{K}}$ after Sylvester horizontal projection:
    $$\text{KrausGauge} = \max_h \frac{\|\Pi_{\mathcal{V}_{\mathbf{K}}}(\Xi_{\mathbf{K},h})\|_F}{\|\Xi_{\mathbf{K},h}\|_F + \varepsilon} \qquad (\text{exact target & observed value: } 0.00\text{e+}00).$$
